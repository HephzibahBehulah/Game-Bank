# Motorcycle Hill Climb verification

1. Launch from the hub. Confirm Red Ridge is available and the remaining authored stages are locked.
2. Complete all eight elevation profiles with throttle, brake, and pitch control; confirm each finish screen and sequential unlock.
3. Approach the marked jump below the required speed and confirm the gap failure. Retry at speed and use lean to control pitch in flight.
4. Hold excessive lean until pitch exceeds 68 degrees and confirm the loop-out result.
5. Confirm best times and unlocked stage persist after leaving and reopening the game.
6. Check D-pad/A/B/Start flow and RT/LT/left-stick behavior with a physical controller when available; keyboard controls offer the same core actions.
