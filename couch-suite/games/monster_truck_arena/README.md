# Monster Truck Arena

A 90-second, six-truck destruction derby with two local drivers and four behavior-driven opponents. Five authored stadiums change their traction and target layout. Smash the arena's named breakable targets for points, use the boost to line up impacts, and launch from the stunt ramp to earn air-time bonuses. Heavy impacts increase truck damage and reduce health; the leaderboard shows score and damage throughout the round. Player one and two can cooperate or compete for the top score.

## Run
Open the Godot 4.7 or newer project at couch-suite/project.godot. Choose Racing → Monster Truck Arena from the hub, or run games/monster_truck_arena.tscn.

## Controls
- Player 1 keyboard: W throttle, A/D steer, S brake, Shift boost.
- Player 2 keyboard: Up throttle, Left/Right steer, Down brake, Enter boost.
- Gamepad: left stick steer, RT throttle, LT brake, RB boost, A/Cross start, Start/Options pause.
- Escape / B returns from the briefing to the hub.

Stadiums, drivers, trucks and objects are fictional. Vehicle behavior is arcade handling, not a real crash or vehicle safety simulation.
