# Stock Car Oval

Six-car stock-car oval racing for one or two local drivers. Race six laps at five authored circuits with different length, banking, grip and drafting strength. Hold a line through the banking, brake when tire temperature rises, and tuck behind traffic to gain a draft. Four rule-driven rival cars hold distinct lanes and throttle patterns. Close contact with a human car damages both cars and bleeds speed. The first human driver to cross the finish wins; the complete field appears in the results.

## Run
Open the Godot 4.7 or newer project at couch-suite/project.godot. Choose Racing → Stock Car Oval from the hub, or run games/stock_car_oval.tscn.

## Controls
- Player 1 keyboard: W throttle, A/D steer, S brake.
- Player 2 keyboard: Up throttle, Left/Right steer, Down brake.
- Gamepad: left stick steer, RT throttle, LT brake, A/Cross confirm, Start/Options pause.
- Escape / B returns from the track briefing to the hub.

Routes, teams and rivals are fictional. Handling is an arcade model, not a certified vehicle or tire simulation.
