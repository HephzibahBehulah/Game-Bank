# Truck Trial playable verification

- [ ] Launch from the hub and confirm Game 4 is enabled while unfinished games remain locked.
- [ ] Complete each of the eight named obstacle courses; keep speed below each hazard guidance and verify the finish screen.
- [ ] Enter obstacles too fast and confirm body roll rises; cross 48 degrees and confirm rollover ends the run.
- [ ] In Crane Yard and Timber Recovery, raise the crane before the marked obstruction and verify the crane-clear message.
- [ ] Verify finishing unlocks only the next course and saves best stage time after reopening.
- [ ] Use A/D-pad/Start on a controller for course selection, start, and pause; verify RT/LT throttle and brake and the right stick crane on crane stages.
